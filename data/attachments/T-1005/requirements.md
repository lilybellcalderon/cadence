# T-1005 Requirements: Retention policy vs. GDPR storage limitation

## Context
An external audit will review our data-retention schedule against GDPR Article 5(1)(e) (storage limitation) and supervisory-authority guidance. The requester wants an Expert-level (level 4) assessment because the output will be shown to the auditors.

## Inputs
- Current retention schedule (47 record categories).
- Data inventory mapping categories to systems.
- Applicable legal retention obligations (tax, employment, health records) already collected by Legal.

## Required assessment
1. For each record category: stated retention period, legal basis, and whether the period is justified.
2. Gaps: categories with no defined period, periods without a stated basis, or periods longer than the justification.
3. Risk rating (High/Medium/Low) per gap with rationale.
4. Recommended remediation and owner.
5. A list of ambiguous points where legal counsel's opinion is needed.

## Deliverable
Obligation-to-control matrix and a gap assessment report with citations to the specific clause or guidance for each finding.

## Note on routing
Minimum expertise level is 4. If no contributor qualifies, the operator decides whether to lower the bar (and add an expert review step), split the task, or find outside help.
