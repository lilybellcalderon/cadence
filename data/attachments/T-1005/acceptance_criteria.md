# T-1005 Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC1: All 47 record categories are assessed; none is skipped without a documented reason.
- [ ] AC2: Every finding cites the specific GDPR article, recital or guidance paragraph it relies on.
- [ ] AC3: Each gap has a risk rating and a rationale that a reviewer can challenge.
- [ ] AC4: Ambiguous points needing legal counsel are listed separately and not presented as conclusions.
- [ ] AC5: The report states clearly what was and was not reviewed.
- [ ] AC6: The requester confirms the report is suitable to hand to the external auditor.
