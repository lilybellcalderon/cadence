# T-1004 Requirements: Customer returns process map

## Context
Average time from return request to refund is 11 days against a target of 5. Support, the warehouse and finance each own a segment; nobody owns the whole.

## Method
1. Interview one person from each team (support, warehouse receiving, finance AR).
2. Trace at least 20 recent returns (mix of fast and slow) from the ticket system, warehouse log and refund log.
3. Record timestamps at each handoff and calculate active time vs. wait time per step.

## Deliverable
- As-is process map (swimlane) with owner per step.
- Table of median and 90th-percentile elapsed time per step.
- The top three delay points with evidence and a suggested change for each.
- Open questions list.

## Out of scope
Implementing the changes; changing return policy.
