# T-1004 Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC1: The map shows every handoff between support, warehouse and finance, with a named owner for each step.
- [ ] AC2: At least 20 real returns were traced and the case IDs are listed in an appendix.
- [ ] AC3: Elapsed time per step is quantified (median and P90) and distinguishes active work from waiting.
- [ ] AC4: The top three delay points together account for the majority of excess time, shown with numbers.
- [ ] AC5: Each delay point has a concrete suggested change and the metric that would show it worked.
- [ ] AC6: The requester can present the map to all three teams without additional explanation.
