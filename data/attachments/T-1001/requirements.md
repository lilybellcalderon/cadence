# T-1001 Requirements: Q3 T&E budget overrun

## Context
Travel & entertainment (T&E) spend for Q3 is above budget. The ledger reports totals by GL account only; the VP of Sales needs to understand the drivers before the quarterly business review (QBR).

## Scope
- All T&E GL accounts (6100-6199) for July-September.
- Compare actuals to the approved Q3 budget and to Q3 of the prior year.
- Break down by cost centre (12 cost centres in Sales).

## Inputs
- Ledger extract of Q3 T&E lines (sample attached: `te_spend_sample.csv`; full extract available on request).
- Approved Q3 budget by cost centre.
- Conference and event calendar for Q3/Q4.

## Required analysis
1. Total variance and variance by cost centre (absolute and %).
2. Split each variance into **timing** (spend booked early/late relative to the budget phasing) and **overspend** (genuinely above the full-year plan).
3. Top 10 individual items driving the variance.
4. One-paragraph commentary per cost centre with a variance above 10%.

## Deliverable
Excel workbook (summary tab, detail tab, assumptions tab) and a one-page written summary suitable for the VP of Sales.

## Out of scope
Policy recommendations on travel rules.
