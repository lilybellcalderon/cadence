# T-1001 Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC1: Variance totals on the summary tab equal the ledger total for accounts 6100-6199 to the cent.
- [ ] AC2: Every cost centre with a variance above 10% has a commentary paragraph naming at least one specific driver.
- [ ] AC3: Each variance is split into timing vs. overspend, and the assumption used to classify an item as timing is written on the assumptions tab.
- [ ] AC4: The top-10 items list shows vendor, date, amount and cost centre, and ties back to ledger line IDs.
- [ ] AC5: The one-page summary can be read in under 3 minutes and states the headline number, the main cause and one recommended next step.
- [ ] AC6: A reviewer can trace any figure in the summary to a cell in the detail tab without asking the analyst.
