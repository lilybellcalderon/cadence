# T-1003 Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC1: Retention curves for all six cohorts are provided with cohort sizes and a stated definition of "retained".
- [ ] AC2: The memo states explicitly whether the metric definition or tracking changed during the period, with the evidence checked.
- [ ] AC3: The onboarding-exposed vs. unexposed comparison is shown, with sample sizes and an appropriate caution about confounders.
- [ ] AC4: At least three alternative drivers are evaluated, each with a stated confidence level.
- [ ] AC5: All queries are saved and reproduce the headline numbers when re-run.
- [ ] AC6: The memo ends with a clear recommendation (roll back / keep / run a controlled test) the requester can act on.
