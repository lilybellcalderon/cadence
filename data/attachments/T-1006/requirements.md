# T-1006 Requirements: Support team onboarding guide

## Audience
New tier-1 support agents in their first week. No prior knowledge of our tools or product.

## Content to cover
1. Day-one setup: accounts, ticket system, chat, knowledge base.
2. Understanding the queues and priorities.
3. Handling the 10 most common ticket types (list supplied by the team lead).
4. When and how to escalate to tier 2.
5. Who to ask for help; working-hours and on-call basics.

## Format
Single document, task-oriented, maximum 12 pages, screenshots where they save words. Published on the team wiki.

## Process
Interview the team lead and two experienced agents. Test the guide with the next new hire and revise.
