# T-1008 Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC1: Every timeline entry has a timestamp and a source; gaps in the timeline are stated, not filled by guesswork.
- [ ] AC2: Impact figures (orders, hours, cost) reconcile with the order-hold report.
- [ ] AC3: The review identifies at least one root cause and two contributing factors without naming individuals as the cause.
- [ ] AC4: Every corrective action has an owner, a due date and a verification method.
- [ ] AC5: The customer-facing summary is under 200 words and reviewed by Customer Experience.
- [ ] AC6: The logistics director confirms the review is ready to present to leadership.
