# T-1009 Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC1: The three existing counts are reproduced for the last full quarter and their overlap is shown.
- [ ] AC2: The proposed definition specifies numerator, denominator, window, grain and exclusions without ambiguity.
- [ ] AC3: At least five edge cases are listed, each with the agreed treatment.
- [ ] AC4: The impact of the new definition on each team's current headline number is quantified.
- [ ] AC5: Representatives of Sales, Finance and Product have reviewed the definition.
